# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

Inline Translator does not collect any personal data for its developer. There is no analytics, tracking, or telemetry, and the developer operates no server.

- Settings (target language, translation service, display style, per-site auto-translate preferences, custom rules, and optional API configuration) are stored locally on your device and never sent to the developer.
- When you translate a page, only the text being translated is sent directly from your browser to the service you select: Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com), Microsoft Edge Translate (edge.microsoft.com), or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure. No page URLs, titles, or browsing history are transmitted. Each service processes the text under its own privacy policy.
- If you use the optional OpenAI-compatible service, your API key is stored locally and sent only to the endpoint you configure, solely to authenticate your translation requests.
- Translations are cached in memory per tab and discarded when the tab closes.

Contact: <https://github.com/ShenMian/inline-translator/issues>
